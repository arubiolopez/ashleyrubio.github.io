Chicken Caesar Wrap

Chicken Caesar Wrap

Time:10-15 minutes

Yield: 4

Allergens: Eggs, Dairy, Gluten

Ingredients:

1 rotisserie chicken

1 tablespoon olive oil

1 teaspoon garlic powder

1 teaspoon paprika

4 large flour tortillas

1 ½ cups Romaine lettuce, chopped

1 cup Caesar dressing

1 cup shredded Parmesan cheese

1 cup croutons, crushed

Video Directions

Written Directions:

1. Shred and season your chicken for added flavor.

2. Warm the tortillas slightly.

3. In a bowl, combine the Romaine lettuce with the Caesar dressing. Combine until the lettuce is well-coated.

4. Lay each tortilla flat on a surface. Place a generous amount of the lettuce in the center of each tortilla. Add a few slices of the cooked chicken on top of the lettuce. Sprinkle shredded Parmesan cheese and crushed croutons.

5. Fold in the sides of the tortilla and then roll it tightly from the bottom to form a wrap.

6. Slice each wrap in half and serve immediately.
